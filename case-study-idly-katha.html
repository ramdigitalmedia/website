Idly Katha: Scaling heritage with digital precision

Transforming a traditional culinary brand into a digital powerhouse through strategic performance marketing and immersive storytelling.

The Challenge

Despite offering authentic, high-quality traditional South Indian cuisine, Idly Katha struggled to translate their offline reputation into online sales. Their digital presence was fragmented, lacking a cohesive narrative that resonated with modern, digitally-native consumers. They needed a strategy to aggressively acquire new customers while retaining their loyal base — all without diluting their rich heritage.

Low Online VisibilityHigh CPA
02

The Strategy

RAM Digital deployed a hyper-targeted, multi-channel performance marketing engine paired with a brand refresh that celebrated the ‘Katha’ (story) behind the food.

Social Media Architecture

Rebuilt Instagram and Facebook profiles as visual menus and storytelling platforms, using high-end food videography and user-generated content loops.

Performance Engines

Implemented dynamic catalog ads on Meta and location-based search campaigns on Google to capture high-intent local delivery traffic.

03

Measurable Impact

The campaign yielded unprecedented growth within the first 90 days, fundamentally shifting reliance from third-party aggregators to owned channels.
