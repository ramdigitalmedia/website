SpicyHub: Redefining the fusion dining experience

How RAM Digital scaled a local favorite into a regional delivery powerhouse through aggressive social commerce and performance marketing.

210%Revenue Growth
4.5xROAS
3.2xDelivery Surge

01

The Challenge

SpicyHub had built a strong reputation as a beloved local walk-in spot known for vibrant fusion flavors. But changing consumer habits demanded a shift. The challenge was transitioning from a purely dine-in model to a delivery-first brand — without sacrificing the premium perception of their food quality or their connection with the local community.

Before

Dine-in focus

Strong local reputation, minimal delivery-app presence, no structured paid acquisition.

02

The Strategy

We architected a multi-channel digital offensive focused on “craving creation.”

Visual Stimulation

Cinematic Reels showcasing the cooking process and sizzle, optimized for Instagram.

Hyper-Local Targeting

Geo-fenced ads for occasions like Father’s Day and family combo promotions.

App Optimization

Revamped DoorDash and UberEats menus for higher conversion.

Creative Execution

Campaign asset

High-impact visual assets designed to stop the scroll and drive immediate action.

03

The Impact

The strategic pivot yielded explosive growth across digital channels, firmly establishing SpicyHub as a dominant player in the regional delivery market.

3.2xDelivery Surge — combined UberEats and DoorDash order volume within 60 days
50k+Monthly Reach across local social media demographics
